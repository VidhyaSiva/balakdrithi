Our philosophy

Three commitments we call the 3 Cs

Everything we do traces back to three things we refuse to compromise on.

Competence in children

Helping every child build the real, everyday life skills they need to thrive on their own terms — not just pass a test.

Confidence in parents

You are your child's first and most important advocate. We work alongside you, not around you — so you know your child can carry forward without you.

Compassion in providers

Every child is met with acceptance, not a search for what to "fix." Their differences are understood, not pathologised.

We don't do eclectic care — a bit of this, a bit of that, whatever seems to work. Every method we use is evidence-based, or we don't use it.

Our services

Comprehensive care, without the runaround

Assessment, intervention, and family support — coordinated under one roof, so you're never left piecing it together alone.

Diagnostic assessment

Structured, standardised evaluation using gold-standard tools like ADOS-2 — clear answers about autism, not vague impressions.

Early intervention (ABA-based)

Individualised, play-driven programmes that build communication, social connection, and everyday independence.

Speech & occupational therapy

Supporting communication, sensory processing, and daily-living skills alongside behavioural intervention.

Parent coaching

Practical, judgement-free guidance so families feel equipped — not just informed — between sessions.

School readiness

Building the skills that determine whether formal schooling actually works for your child — on their timeline, not the calendar's.

Referral partnerships

Working directly with paediatricians and neuro-paediatricians for the fastest possible path from concern to care.
