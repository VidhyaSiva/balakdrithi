What we offer

  • Assessment: a structured, standardised evaluation, so you get clear answers about your child and what to do next.
  • Early intervention (ABA): individualised, play-based programs that build communication, social connection and everyday independence. What is ABA?
  • Speech and occupational therapy: communication, sensory processing and daily-living skills, alongside ABA in the same day.
  • Parent coaching: practical, judgement-free guidance so you can support your child at home.
  • School readiness: the skills that make formal schooling work for your child, on their timeline.

Is this for my child?

Families often contact us when their child isn't talking yet or has few words at 2 or 3, doesn't respond to their name, plays in repeated ways, finds change very hard, or has recently been diagnosed with autism. You don't need a diagnosis to reach out.

How we work. We use assent-based ABA: we ask for your child's cooperation and never demand it. Every method we use is evidence-based, and every therapist trains for about 40 hours and shadows an expert for two weeks before working with a child alone.

Which ages do you work with?

Children aged 2 to 8. Early intervention matters most in these years, so this is the only age group we serve.

Do I need an autism diagnosis to contact you?

No. Many families come to us with a worry, not a diagnosis: a child who isn't talking yet, doesn't respond to their name, or finds change very hard. We will listen and help you find the right next step, even if it isn't us.

What is ABA therapy?

Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) teaches communication, play, self-care and other everyday skills in small, achievable steps, and measures progress week by week. We use assent-based ABA: we ask for your child's cooperation and never demand it.

Is it a full-day program or weekly sessions?

Balak Drithi is a full-day early intervention program on weekdays, with ABA, occupational therapy and speech therapy under one roof, so your child doesn't have to travel between clinics.

How many children does each therapist work with?

Never more than 4 children per therapist, so every child gets real, focused time. Every therapist completes about 40 hours of training and two weeks of shadowing before working with a child on their own.

How will I know how my child is doing?

You get daily notes from your child's therapist, and a senior clinician reviews your child's progress every one to two weeks. We also coach parents, because progress at home matters as much as progress at the academy.

What are the fees?

Fees depend on the program your child needs. Contact us and we will explain them clearly before you decide anything.
