What sets us apart

Five pillars behind every child’s progress

The commitments that shape how we hire, train, measure and stay in touch with you.

01

A science-based model

+

Evidence-based ABA, never guesswork, with never more than 4 children per therapist, so your child gets real, focused time.

02

World-class training

+

Every therapist completes about 40 hours of training, then spends two weeks shadowing an expert before working with a child on their own.

03

Accountable to children and parents

+

We benchmark ourselves against India’s best centres, and hold ourselves accountable for your child’s outcomes and for your trust.

04

You’re never in the dark

+

Daily notes from your child’s therapist, and a senior clinician reviews your child’s progress every one to two weeks.

05

Getting it right in Chennai first

+

We’re proving our quality in one Chennai centre, measured by families who stay with us, before we open anywhere else.

Our services

Comprehensive care, without the runaround

Assessment, intervention, and family support, all under one roof.

Diagnostic assessment

Structured, standardised evaluation using gold-standard tools like ADOS-2. Clear answers, not vague impressions.

Early intervention (ABA-based)

Individualised, play-driven programmes that build communication, social connection, and everyday independence.

Speech & occupational therapy

Supporting communication, sensory processing, and daily-living skills alongside behavioural intervention.

Parent coaching

Practical, judgement-free guidance so families feel equipped, not just informed, between sessions.

School readiness

Building the skills that make formal schooling work for your child, on their timeline.

Referral partnerships

Working directly with paediatricians and neuro-paediatricians for the fastest possible path from concern to care.
